## 4. Architectural Decisions
*   **Physics Propagation Strategy:** I decided to attach physics properties (amplifiers, port loss) directly to the domain models (`FiberLink` generated by the testbed client) and propagate them through the solver. This creates a clean, one-way data flow without requiring side-lookups in the QoT module.
*   **Semantic Divergence ($d_{sem}$):** I opted to use a fast, judge-based LLM call to score semantic agreement between the original intent and the reconstruction on a `0.0-1.0` scale. This avoids introducing a heavy `sentence-transformers` dependency while satisfying the evaluation needs for the thesis.
*   **Separation of Concerns in HITL:** I removed the 3-way routing (`approve`/`refine`/`reject`) from the [[architecture/features/reverse_prompt]] node. The node now solely handles reconstruction and the LangGraph `interrupt()`, while the [[architecture/features/semantic_gate]] owns the routing decision based on the $\tau_{sem}$ threshold.
